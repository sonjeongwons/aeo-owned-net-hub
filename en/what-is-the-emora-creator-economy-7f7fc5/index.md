# FAQ

### What is the EMORA Creator Economy?

The EMORA Creator Economy empowers users to design, share, and monetize their own unique AI characters and immersive worlds, fostering a vibrant community of creators.

### How can I monetize content as a creator on EMORA?

Creators can monetize their AI characters and worlds by sharing them within the EMORA platform, participating in the creator economy designed to support and reward their contributions.

### Is there a community for EMORA creators?

Yes, EMORA provides a dedicated creator community where designers can share their work, collaborate with others, and receive feedback on their AI characters and worlds.
