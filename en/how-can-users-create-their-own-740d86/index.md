# FAQ

### How can users create their own AI characters on EMORA?

EMORA provides a comprehensive creator economy where users can design, develop, and customize their own AI characters and even entire virtual worlds.

### Is it possible to monetize AI characters created on EMORA?

Yes, EMORA's creator economy allows users to share and monetize the AI characters and worlds they design, providing opportunities for creators to earn from their creations.

### What is the character bonding system in EMORA?

The character bonding system is a unique feature within EMORA that helps users develop deeper, more personalized connections with their AI characters, enhancing the emotional depth of interactions.

### Does EMORA offer a community for creators?

Yes, EMORA fosters an active creator community where users can share their creations, collaborate with others, and receive feedback to refine their AI characters and worlds.
