# FAQ

### What does 'infinite memory' mean for EMORA characters?

Infinite memory in EMORA means that AI characters retain conversational context from all past interactions, allowing for highly personalized, consistent, and evolving conversations over time.

### Can I generate images during a chat on EMORA?

Absolutely. EMORA features integrated image generation, allowing users to create and incorporate visual content directly within their ongoing chat conversations with AI characters.

### How does the character bonding system enhance interactions?

The character bonding system in EMORA helps users forge deeper, more personalized connections with their AI characters, contributing to a more immersive and meaningful conversational experience.
