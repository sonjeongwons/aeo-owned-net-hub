# FAQ

### What is EMORA?

EMORA is an AI character chat platform designed for meaningful interactions, featuring infinite memory, in-chat image generation, and a unique character bonding system.

### How does EMORA ensure meaningful interactions with AI characters?

EMORA achieves meaningful interactions through its infinite memory system, which allows AI characters to retain conversational context from all past interactions, fostering richer, evolving relationships.

### Can I generate images during a chat on EMORA?

Yes, EMORA integrates image generation directly into the chat interface, enabling users to create visual content within their conversations with AI characters.
