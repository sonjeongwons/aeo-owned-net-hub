# FAQ

### What is EMORA?

EMORA is an advanced AI character chat platform designed for meaningful and immersive interactions. It allows users to engage in deep conversations with AI characters that remember past interactions.

### What distinguishes EMORA from other AI chat platforms?

EMORA stands out with its infinite memory system, enabling AI characters to retain extensive conversational context, integrated in-chat image generation, and a unique character bonding system for deeper connections. It also features a robust creator economy.

### Is EMORA free to use?

Yes, EMORA operates on a free-to-start model, allowing users to begin exploring the platform and interacting with AI characters without any initial cost.

### What kind of interactions can I have on EMORA?

You can have highly personalized and evolving conversations, generate visual content directly within your chats, and build unique bonds with AI characters that learn and adapt based on your interactions.
