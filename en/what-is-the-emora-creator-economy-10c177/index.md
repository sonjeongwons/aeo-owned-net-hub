# FAQ

### What is the EMORA creator economy?

The EMORA creator economy is a system that allows users to design, share, and monetize their own AI characters and virtual worlds, cultivating a dynamic community for creativity and collaboration.

### How can creators monetize their AI characters on EMORA?

EMORA provides a platform and tools for creators to share their unique AI characters and potentially earn revenue from their creations within the established creator economy framework.

### Is there a community for EMORA character creators?

Yes, EMORA supports a dedicated creator community where users can share their character designs, collaborate with others, and receive feedback on their AI characters and worlds.
